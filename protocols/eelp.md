# EELP Integration

## Role within the Framework

EELP—the Ethical Experience Lab Protocol—is the Playa AI Living Trust Framework's field implementation protocol for AI-mediated events, installations, workshops, research activities and participatory experiences.

The Living Trust Framework provides the stewardship architecture. EELP specifies operational conditions for experiences involving people, stories, identity, memory, voice or other sensitive participation.

## Current EELP focus

EELP is being developed to make the following practices reviewable:

- Clear disclosure of AI participation and system purpose
- Separate consent layers for participation, recording, research, publication and model training
- Data minimization, retention limits and deletion workflows
- Meaningful human override and accountable escalation
- Accessible choices and non-AI alternatives where practicable
- Public registration of approved uses and honest limitations
- Evidence-backed self-assessment without certification claims

## Boundary between protocol and profile

- Use **EELP** to govern the participant experience and field operation.
- Use the **Data Stewardship Profile** to govern personal and relational data across systems, vendors and lifecycle stages.
- Apply both when an AI-mediated experience collects, infers, stores, shares or trains on participant data.

## Status

EELP remains a public-review candidate and is not ratified or certified. Its working repository is being prepared separately. Until a stable public release exists, adopters must cite the exact EELP version or commit reviewed and must not claim certification or project endorsement.
