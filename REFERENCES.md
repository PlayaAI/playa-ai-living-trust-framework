# Reference Register

**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-15

This register favors primary legal authorities, regulators, standards bodies, and source organizations. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, legal applicability, equivalence, or conformance. Framework mappings will be published only after control-by-control review.

## Collective and Indigenous data governance

- Global Indigenous Data Alliance, [CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance](https://www.gida-global.org/care)

CARE is not a checklist to apply to Indigenous data without participation. Work involving Indigenous peoples, knowledge, lands, or data should be led or governed by the relevant peoples and respect their laws, protocols, authority, and benefit priorities.

## Review rules

When proposing a new authority or mapping:

1. Identify the exact version, jurisdiction, publication date, and access date.
2. Link to the primary source and preserve a stable identifier when available.
3. Distinguish a legal requirement from guidance, ethics, or project policy.
4. Record exceptions, definitions, and scope rather than matching on keywords.
5. Obtain qualified review before presenting a jurisdictional mapping as reliable.
