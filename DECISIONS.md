# Decision Register

Material governance and architecture decisions are recorded here so future contributors can understand what changed, why, and under whose authority.

## D-001 — Separate research, charter, standard, and templates

**Status:** Proposed in v0.2 draft

**Date:** 2026-07-15

**Decision-maker:** Interim maintainer

### Context

The initial public draft combined comparative AI-generated research, philosophical commitments, operational promises, legal rights language, implementation notes, and repository planning in one README. Readers could not reliably distinguish provenance, aspiration, normative requirements, or deployable disclosures.

### Decision

The repository will maintain four distinct layers:

1. Research and provenance
2. Constitutional values in the charter
3. Auditable requirements in the standard and control catalog
4. Adaptable implementation and notice templates

### Consequences

- The original synthesis remains preserved and citable.
- The README becomes a concise project landing page.
- Normative changes require synchronized prose and machine-readable updates.
- Templates cannot be represented as complete or legally sufficient without adaptation and review.

## D-002 — Do not launch certification during the formation stage

**Status:** Proposed in v0.2 draft

**Date:** 2026-07-15

**Decision-maker:** Interim maintainer

### Context

Certification without stable controls, assessor independence, appeals, surveillance, revocation, funding safeguards, and pilot evidence would create false assurance and capture risk.

### Decision

The project will support documented, implemented, verified, and exception evidence states but prohibit certification or endorsement claims until public conformance and assurance governance is ratified.

### Consequences

- Early adopters can report exact scope and evidence without overstating approval.
- Pilot findings can shape requirements before an assurance market develops.
