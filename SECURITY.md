# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, exposed credential, private repository data, or personal data.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository:

https://github.com/PlayaAI/playa-ai-privacy-toolkit/security/advisories/new

If that channel is unavailable, contact the repository owner privately through the `@PlayaAI` GitHub profile and request a secure reporting channel. Do not send sensitive evidence until a suitable channel is confirmed.

Include, when safe:

- Affected file, tool, release, or commit
- Reproduction steps or proof of concept
- Likely impact and affected people
- Suggested mitigation
- Whether data or credentials may already be exposed
- Your preferred attribution and disclosure timeline

## What to expect

The project aims to acknowledge complete reports within 5 business days, provide an initial assessment within 10 business days, and coordinate remediation and disclosure according to risk. These are service targets, not guaranteed legal commitments.

## Scope

Security reports may concern repository automation, released tooling, schemas that create unsafe defaults, supply-chain integrity, or accidental exposure of sensitive information.

Policy disagreements, legal interpretations, and non-confidential privacy-design concerns should use ordinary issues. Harassment and community-safety matters follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Good-faith research

The project supports good-faith research that avoids privacy harm, service disruption, extortion, unnecessary data access, and premature disclosure. This statement does not authorize testing of third-party systems or waive rights belonging to others.

## Supported versions

Until the first numbered toolkit release, only the latest commit on `main` is supported. Historical research drafts are preserved for provenance and are not maintained implementation guidance.
