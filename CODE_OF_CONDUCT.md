# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a rigorous, inclusive environment where people can challenge ideas without demeaning people. Privacy work often involves unequal power, lived harm, legal disagreement, and cultural context; participation must be safe enough for those realities to be heard.

## Expected conduct

- Engage arguments and evidence in good faith
- Respect names, pronouns, identities, cultures, accessibility needs, and boundaries
- State expertise and uncertainty honestly
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest
- Protect confidential information and personal data
- Make space for people directly affected by the issue
- Accept correction and maintainer decisions without retaliation

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Publishing or soliciting private information without permission
- Deliberate intimidation or disruption
- Fabricating credentials, citations, consensus, implementations, or review
- Using project participation to imply endorsement or pressure vulnerable people
- Retaliation against good-faith reporting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions; issue warnings; restrict participation; or ban participants when reasonably necessary to protect the project and community. Enforcement should consider severity, pattern, power imbalance, safety, and the possibility of repair.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not expose sensitive conduct reports in public issues. The maintainer will disclose conflicts and seek an independent reviewer when practicable.

Good-faith criticism of the project, its maintainers, funders, adopters, or governance is not harassment.
