# Contributing

Contributions are welcome from affected communities, privacy and security practitioners, lawyers, engineers, researchers, designers, translators, and implementers.

## Before contributing

- Read the [charter](CHARTER.md), [governance policy](GOVERNANCE.md), and [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Search existing issues and pull requests.
- Do not include personal data, confidential client information, credentials, vulnerability details, or privileged legal advice.
- For material changes, open an issue before investing in a large pull request.

## Useful contributions

- Implementation reports with concrete evidence and failure modes
- Corrections supported by primary legal or technical sources
- Control language that is testable across different organization sizes
- Threat models, deletion tests, consent receipts, and data-inventory tooling
- Jurisdictional overlays reviewed by qualified local counsel
- Accessibility review and carefully reviewed translations
- Research on collective, Indigenous, post-mortem, and children's data governance led by affected people

## Normative drafting rules

The standard uses these terms:

- **MUST / MUST NOT:** required for the stated conformance profile
- **SHOULD / SHOULD NOT:** expected unless a documented reason justifies a different control
- **MAY:** optional

A normative proposal should include:

1. The problem and people affected
2. Proposed text and affected control IDs
3. Evidence that could demonstrate implementation
4. Legal, technical, accessibility, and equity implications
5. Costs and impact on small organizations
6. Alternatives considered
7. Known limitations and open questions

Avoid universal legal claims. State the jurisdiction, effective date, authority, and relevant exceptions. Prefer legislation, regulators, standards bodies, and peer-reviewed research over marketing summaries.

## Pull requests

- Keep each pull request focused.
- Update prose, machine-readable controls, templates, and changelog together when applicable.
- Preserve stable control identifiers.
- Use clear commit messages and explain validation performed.
- Mark generated or substantially AI-assisted material and identify the human review completed.

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit the work under the repository's applicable license. The project does not currently require a contributor license agreement.

## Reviews

Review is evidence-seeking, not status-seeking. Identify whether feedback is legal analysis, engineering experience, lived experience, research interpretation, or editorial judgment. No single review establishes compliance or correctness.
