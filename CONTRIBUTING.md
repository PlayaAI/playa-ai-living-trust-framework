# Contributing

This repository is in public review. Contributions may identify errors, missing perspectives, implementation risks, evidence, or questions for future development.

## Before contributing

- Search existing issues and pull requests.
- Open an issue before preparing a substantial change.
- Do not include personal data, confidential information, credentials, vulnerability details, culturally restricted material, or privileged legal advice.
- State whether a contribution reflects legal analysis, technical experience, research interpretation, lived experience, or editorial judgment.
- Cite primary sources where practical.
- Identify material conflicts of interest.
- Mark substantially AI-assisted material and describe the human review completed.

## Important boundaries

A submission does not become an adopted requirement merely because it is accepted for discussion or merged. It does not establish legal approval, certification, compliance, endorsement, community validation, or implementation by Playa AI.

Because the repository's license remains under review, contributors should not assume that submitted or existing material carries an open-source or content license. License and contribution terms must be confirmed before accepting substantive third-party material.

## How to participate

Open an issue for public-review feedback or email [hello@playa-ai.org](mailto:hello@playa-ai.org) for questions that should not be posted publicly. Do not report security vulnerabilities or sensitive personal information in a public issue.
