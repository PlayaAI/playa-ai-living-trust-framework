# Preliminary Brand and Name Review

**Review date:** 2026-07-15  
**Proposed name:** The Playa AI Living Trust Framework  
**Fallback name:** Playa AI Community AI Stewardship Framework

## Status

This is a documented preliminary knockout review, not a legal opinion, trademark clearance or assurance that the proposed name is available. Mystic Arts Foundation should obtain counsel's written clearance before the name is treated as exclusively owned, registered or final for a stable release.

## Scope reviewed

The preliminary review considered:

- USPTO Trademark Search results for combinations of `Living Trust`, `Trust Framework`, `Playa AI` and the proposed full name
- General web and publication usage of the proposed and fallback phrases
- GitHub repository naming and code-search conflicts
- The proposed project domains and route structure
- California business-name searching as a required legal follow-up

The repository slug `PlayaAI/playa-ai-living-trust-framework` was available when checked. That availability does not create trademark rights.

## Preliminary findings

1. No identical combined mark for **Playa AI Living Trust Framework** was observed in the preliminary searches performed.
2. `Living Trust` is heavily used for estate-planning and legal services. The proposed name therefore needs the non-legal-trust clarification wherever a reader could mistake the Framework for a legal vehicle.
3. USPTO results include marks and applications containing `trust framework` and many marks containing `living trust`, across different classes and statuses. Similarity, related services, geography and common-law use require professional analysis; an exact-match search is insufficient.
4. The distinctiveness of the full name depends materially on `Playa AI` and the cultural/community stewardship context. The project should not claim exclusivity in the ordinary terms `living trust`, `framework`, `community AI` or `stewardship` by themselves.
5. GitHub and domain availability are operational signals only. They do not resolve federal, state or common-law trademark risk.

## Required clearance gate

Before PR #1 is marked ready or a stable release is branded, counsel should:

1. Run and document a professional knockout and full search covering USPTO records, California entities, relevant domains, GitHub, publications, social platforms and common-law usage.
2. Compare the goods and services, channels, audiences, geographic reach, dates of first use and overall commercial impression of potentially similar names.
3. Review ownership and licensing of `Playa AI` and the relationship between Mystic Arts Foundation, Playa AI, On Our Own Terms and this resource.
4. Approve the clarification below and advise on trademark symbols, attribution and any registration strategy.
5. Record an approval decision in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) or replace the name with the predetermined fallback.

> “Living Trust” is the name of an evolving community-governance framework. It is not an estate-planning trust, fiduciary arrangement, certification or separate legal entity.

## Decision rule

- **If cleared:** retain **The Playa AI Living Trust Framework** and use the subtitle consistently.
- **If the conflict is manageable only with conditions:** document those conditions before public launch.
- **If counsel identifies an unacceptable conflict:** use **Playa AI Community AI Stewardship Framework** without reopening the naming process.

Until the gate is complete, the v0.2 work remains a draft public-consultation foundation. The project must not assert registration, exclusive rights, certification or legal-trust status.
