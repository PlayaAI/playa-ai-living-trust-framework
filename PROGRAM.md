# On Our Own Terms

## Program brief

**On Our Own Terms is a Mystic Arts Foundation program delivered through Playa AI. The Playa AI Living Trust Framework is one of its openly licensed community resources.**

## Community challenge

AI is increasingly present in artistic creation, cultural programming, archives, community gatherings and public storytelling. Artists and cultural organizations often lack practical, participatory ways to decide how these systems should enter their spaces, whose work and stories may be used, what meaningful consent requires, and how a community can object or change course.

## Practical intervention

On Our Own Terms centers community agency through:

- AI literacy and stewardship workshops
- Artist-led experiments in responsible AI practice
- Community dialogues about acceptable and unacceptable uses
- Field testing of consent, disclosure and human-override practices
- Adaptation and testing of the Playa AI Living Trust Framework
- Openly licensed resources that cultural organizations can reuse

## Outputs

1. Workshop and dialogue materials
2. Documented artist-led experiments
3. Community findings, including dissent and unresolved questions
4. The Playa AI Living Trust Framework and Data Stewardship Profile
5. EELP implementation guidance for AI-mediated experiences
6. Practical templates and playbooks for cultural organizations

## Intended outcomes

- Artists and cultural organizations can make more informed AI decisions.
- Community members gain meaningful influence over AI in cultural spaces.
- Consent, disclosure, authorship, privacy and human agency become visible operating practices.
- Small organizations gain adaptable resources without needing to invent governance from scratch.
- Field learning returns to the public commons without exposing participants or appropriating community knowledge.

## Scale and public benefit

The program is designed to produce reusable methods rather than impose one universal cultural answer. Each adopter should preserve local authority and document adaptations. Scale means more communities can govern AI on their own terms—not that one institution decides for all of them.

## Grant-facing description

> A key output of On Our Own Terms will be the adaptation and testing of the Playa AI Living Trust Framework, an open community AI stewardship resource that helps artists, builders and cultural organizations navigate consent, disclosure, authorship, privacy, participation and human agency when introducing AI into creative and cultural spaces. The Framework will be refined through artist and community engagement and shared openly so other cultural organizations can adapt it to their own contexts.
